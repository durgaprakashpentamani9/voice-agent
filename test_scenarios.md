# Test scenarios

| No. | Scenario | Customer lines to speak | Expected agent behaviour |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perfect eligible customer | “Yeah, I’m free to talk. It’s my residential house, solely owned, and the original papers are at home. I need fifty lakh, I’m salaried and get paid in the bank, it’s worth about one crore, and ten years would work for me.”<br>“Yes, that’s right.” | Verify first, present the loyalty offer, capture all seven items even when volunteered together, clarify only anything unclear, then hand off after all seven pass. |
| 2 | Agricultural property | “Umm, the property is agricultural land.”<br>“No, it’s only used for farming.” | Immediately and politely disqualify, ask no further checklist questions, and end. |
| 3 | Cash-only income | “I run a small business, and all my income comes in cash.”<br>“I don’t receive any income in my bank.” | Treat cash-only income as a clear failure; politely disqualify and end without further questions. |
| 4 | No original documents | “I only have photocopies. The original papers aren’t available.”<br>“I can’t get the originals.” | Immediately disqualify and end. |
| 5 | Tenure 2 years | “I’d need the loan for two years.”<br>“That’s the full period I want.” | Immediately disqualify because tenure is below the allowed range. |
| 6 | Tenure 20 years | “Could I take it for twenty years?”<br>“Yeah, twenty.” | Immediately disqualify because tenure is above the allowed range. |
| 7 | Loan 90 Lakhs, accepts 75 | “I was thinking around ninety lakh rupees.”<br>“Okay, I can go ahead with seventy-five lakh.” | Explain the maximum and ask whether to proceed at that amount; on yes, record the maximum and continue the remaining checklist. |
| 8 | Loan 90 Lakhs, refuses 75 | “I need ninety lakh, really.”<br>“No, seventy-five won’t work for me.” | Explain the maximum and ask whether to proceed; on no, end politely without continuing. |
| 9 | Joint ownership | “The flat is jointly owned with my wife.”<br>“We both own it.” | Record Joint as eligible and continue to the earliest missing item; do not reject joint ownership. |
| 10 | Existing loan plus disqualifying answer | “There’s already a loan against this same property, and it’s agricultural land.”<br>“Yes, both things are true.” | Transfer takes priority over the disqualifying answer in the same turn: skip the eligibility checklist, give the specialist message, and end. |
| 11 | Wants to reduce EMI | “I already have a property loan and want my EMI reduced.”<br>“Can someone help me transfer the balance?” | Trigger transfer on the EMI-reduction/balance-transfer request, give the specialist message, and end without asking the seven items. |
| 12 | Busy customer | “Sorry, I’m in a meeting right now.”<br>“Could you call tomorrow after four in the afternoon?” | Stop the pitch, confirm the callback time using current date/time context, and end. |
| 13 | Out-of-order answers | “It’s a residential flat, jointly owned with my wife, worth about one point two crore.”<br>“The originals are in our locker.” | Capture property, ownership, and market value; ask only the earliest missing item, documents, and do not re-ask captured facts. |
| 14 | Many answers and mixed income in one reply | “It’s a commercial shop, mine alone, the original papers are at home, I want forty lakh, and I’m self-employed. I get some income in cash and some in the bank.”<br>“It’s worth about eighty lakh and I’d like eight years.” | Extract all facts. Ask once whether income is received in bank; store the answer and proceed accordingly. Do not repeat other answered items. |
| 15 | Correction: 10, then 12 years | “I think ten years… umm, actually make that twelve years.”<br>“Yes, twelve is correct.” | Store the latest clear answer, twelve years, and continue without re-asking tenure. |
| 16 | Contradiction: residential, then agricultural | “It’s a residential property—no, sorry, I mean agricultural land.”<br>“Agricultural, yes.” | Latest clear answer wins; immediately disqualify and end. |
| 17 | Interest rate question | “Before I answer, what interest rate will I get?”<br>“And is there an EMI estimate?” | Give no number; say the senior loan expert can share exact details after the preliminary check, then return to the earliest missing question. |
| 18 | Interruption mid-question | “Sorry to interrupt, who are you and why are you calling me?”<br>“Okay, go ahead.” | Briefly identify as an AI assistant from the configured company calling an existing customer about a special offer; then resume at the same missing item. |
| 19 | Plot and ambiguous documents | “It’s a plot, I think. The original papers are somewhere at home, maybe.”<br>“Residential. But I’m not sure whether they’re the originals.” | Ask whether the plot is residential, commercial, or agricultural; clarify original-document availability when it becomes the next missing item. Do not guess either answer. |
| 20 | Skipped question after diversion | “The property is a residential house, jointly owned with my brother.”<br>“Before I answer about the original documents, what’s the interest rate?”<br>After the answer: “Okay, the original papers are at home.” | Answer without a rate, return to the checklist, capture property and ownership, record documents, and ask only the next missing item before any handoff. |
| 21 | Not interested | “No thanks, I’m not interested in this offer.”<br>“Please don’t continue.” | Thank the customer and end; at most one gentle attempt, never push. |
| 22 | Wrong person answers | “This is her brother. She’s not here.”<br>“You can try calling her tomorrow morning.” | Share no offer or customer details; ask for the right person or callback time, confirm the time if given, and end. |
| 23 | Full Hindi call | “जी, मैं बात कर सकता हूँ। यह मेरा घर है, और मूल कागज़ मेरे पास हैं।”<br>“मैं नौकरी करता हूँ और वेतन बैंक में आता है।” | Speak simple Hindi in Devanagari according to configured language; use gendered Hindi forms that strictly match configured agent gender; capture facts and proceed one question at a time. |
| 24 | Pushback after rejection | “It’s agricultural, but surely you can make an exception?”<br>“Please, just approve it anyway.” | Calmly explain that the criteria cannot be changed, do not negotiate or continue questions, and end politely. |
